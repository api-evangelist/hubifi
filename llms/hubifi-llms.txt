# HubiFi

> HubiFi is a revenue recognition and order-to-cash automation platform for high-volume SaaS and subscription businesses. It automates ASC 606 / IFRS 15 compliant revenue recognition, reconciliation, and daily financial close across billing, payment, and ERP systems.

HubiFi is built for finance and accounting teams (Controllers, VPs of Finance, Heads of Revenue Accounting) at high-growth, high-transaction-volume SaaS companies who need GAAP-compliant, audit-ready revenue accounting without adding headcount. Customers include Cursor, ElevenLabs, Perplexity, Miro, and AllTrails.
